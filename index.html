The Precision Advantage
  • Individual Crop Care: Focuses on the precise moisture needs of single plants rather than making assumptions about the entire field.
  • Preventing Extremes: Actively prevents scenarios where one crop is accidentally flooded while the adjacent crop is in a drought.
Tasheen Alam
"Right now there are many Smart irrigation systems in the market but we can bet nothing is like this one, because all of them just focus on whether the whole feild is nicely irrigated not that a single crop is flooded or in drought."
Off-Grid Power
  • 2S2P Lithium-Ion Battery Configuration
  • Monocrystalline Solar Panels
  • Integrated Smart Charge Controller
Saleha Samreen
"A farm robot isn't very useful if it requires constant recharging from a wall outlet. That's why we made it completely solar-powered. It charges itself while working out in the sun, so it can stay in the fields indefinitely."
Under the Hood: Functionality & Analysis

Pros & Cons

  • Pros: Extreme water efficiency, zero electricity cost, and fully autonomous operation.
  • Cons: Higher initial setup cost for the hardware, and charging speed relies on sunny weather.
Microcontroller (Brain)
Processes sensor data and commands the motors.
Capacitive Moisture Sensor
Probes the soil to detect exact hydration levels.
Servos & Water Pump
Deploys the arm and dispenses precise water amounts.
Prathameshwar
"Moving on to how it actually ticks. We use a microcontroller as the central brain to read data from soil moisture sensors. It then commands the servo arm and pump. The biggest pros are huge water savings and autonomy. The main cons are the initial build cost and weather dependency, but the long-term benefits easily outweigh them."
